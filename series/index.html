The Kingdom Journey

Two kingdoms. One King.

The earthly throne God swore to David and the spiritual Kingdom Christ opened — traced side by side, on one map, from Samuel and from the Cross until the two roads meet at a City with twelve gates and twelve foundations. Each week opens with a parable, because the parables are Jesus revealing His own Kingdom.

This one comes with a companion: the map, the week's study, quizzes, your own notes, polls, a community, and a private line to the teacher's desk. There's a place on the road with your name on it — you'll see it once you join.
