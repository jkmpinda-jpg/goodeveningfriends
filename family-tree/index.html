TRIBE VIEW

“These all died in faith, not having received the promises, but having seen them afar off… and confessed that they were strangers and pilgrims on the earth.”Hebrews 11:13
Every name here is a real person in the Book. The gold rail runs down the throne to the King of the Jews; the blue rail runs down the faith-seed — through David’s son Nathan — to Mary. They meet in one Person. The little figures are placeholders: each name is being given its own character.
