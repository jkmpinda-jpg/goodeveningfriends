How to read it

Every name on the tree is a real person in the Bible. The gold rail runs down the throne — Judah, Pharez, Boaz, Jesse, David, Solomon and the kings — to the Man Pilate called the King of the Jews. The blue rail runs down the faith-seed — Abel, Seth, Enoch, Noah, Shem, Abraham, Isaac, Jacob — and, through David's son Nathan, to Mary. The two rails meet in one Person. Names in the dim red are people who walked away from God, and the Bible says so; the rest are the family, kept for the record, because the record is the point.

Two genealogies, one Son

Matthew 1 walks the throne from Abraham down through Solomon to Joseph, the legal father — the King's claim to David's throne. Luke 3 walks the blood back up through Nathan to David, Abraham and Adam — the Son of Man's claim to the whole human family. We show both, and we mark where readers differ on Luke's line; the Bible leaves the two lists side by side without apology, and so do we. The ages and dates are the ones Genesis gives; where the text gives none, we give none.
