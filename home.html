Good evening, friends.

A Bible study channel — the whole Book

This is Bible teaching — the systematic explanation of Scripture, of doctrine, and of how to live it out: not only what is true, but how and why it is true, so that disciples are made and believers grow up. Every lesson is prepared before it's taught: Scripture first, history where it helps, and an honest “unresolved” where the text leaves it open. Yes, even the genealogies. Especially the genealogies.

No hype, no shortcuts, no pulpit voice. Just the Book, broken down — how it is true and why — so you can follow it and check it yourself. Bring your Bible, bring your questions, and bring the friend who thinks they've heard it all — we love those.
This is our story: because of Christ we came from God, and through Christ we are going back to God. Pilgrims and strangers on the road — overcoming sin, death, lust and the powers of this world by the straight and narrow way. Few find it.
