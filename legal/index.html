Community conduct

“Let your communication be, Yea, yea; Nay, nay: for whatsoever is more than these cometh of evil.”Matthew 5:37

This is a Bible study, and it is a journey we walk together. Everyone who keeps these rules is welcome on it — believer, seeker, sceptic or critic. Disagreement is welcome; put it in the open, where everyone can see it, and say it with respect. Questions are welcome. Pushback is welcome. What is not welcome, anywhere on this site:

  • attacks on a person rather than an idea — insults, mockery, contempt, threats, harassment, or singling anyone out;
  • profanity, slurs, sexual content, nudity, violence, or anything a child should not see;
  • spam, advertising, fundraising, recruiting for other platforms, or repeatedly posting the same thing;
  • impersonating any person, the teacher, or the ministry; sharing anyone's private information;
  • trolling, baiting, derailing, or using the rooms to campaign against the faith this site exists to teach — you may disagree with what is taught, on the page where it is taught, in good faith; you may not use the site as a platform against it;
  • anything unlawful, or anything that infringes someone else's rights.

Moderation is at the teacher's discretion. Messages, pictures, profiles and reactions may be hidden, edited for safety, or removed; accounts may be restricted, suspended or removed — with or without notice, and without the obligation to explain. Pictures posted by members are screened before anyone else sees them and may be declined. Participation in the community is a privilege the owner grants and may withdraw; there is no right to a platform here. We may keep a copy of removed content for safety and for the record.

If you see something that breaks these rules, flag it from the message itself. If it concerns you personally, write to us.

Privacy — the short version

You sign in with Google. We keep what you give us and what you make here: your name, email address and (if you keep it) profile picture; your phone number only if you ask for the daily scripture by text; your preferences; your quiz scores and notes; your messages, reactions, Bars and any pictures you post; and when you joined and were last here. It is stored in Google Firebase (Firestore) under our account, and used only to run the site, to send you the updates you opted into, and to keep the community safe.

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with advertisers. Email and text reminders go only to people who opted in, and you can turn them off any time in My Journey. The site uses your browser's local storage for your preferences and a session marker; there is no advertising tracking. Our hosting provider keeps ordinary server logs.

You can download everything you have saved, and delete your account, from My Journey. Deleting removes your profile, scores, notes and sign-in. Things you posted in public rooms may remain as part of the conversation's record; write to us and we will remove your name from them or take them down.
